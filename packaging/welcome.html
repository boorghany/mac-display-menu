Mac Display Menu

The Windows Win+P experience on your Mac, and a single-window mode on top.

Press ⌘P anywhere to choose how your external monitor is used:

  1. Extend
  2. Mirror for the MacBook
  3. Mirror for the monitor
  4. Extend one window: the monitor shows only the window you pick

The app starts at login and has no Dock icon. It needs no Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording permission.
